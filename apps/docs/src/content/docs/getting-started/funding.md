Trades on Rushr are paid in **ETH on Robinhood Chain** (chain id **4663**). USDG on Robinhood Chain works too. You also need a little ETH for gas.

Open **Wallet & deposit** and click **Deposit** to see your address and the ways to add funds.

:::caution
Only send ETH or USDG **on Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663)**. Funds sent on another network won't arrive.
:::

## Ways to add funds

| Option | How |
| --- | --- |
| **From Robinhood** (easiest) | In the Robinhood app, buy ETH or USDG, tap **Send**, choose the **Robinhood Chain** network and paste your Rushr address. Not available in New York. |
| **Bridge from Base, Arbitrum or Ethereum** | **Bridge ETH (Relay)** or **Bridge USDG (Relay)** opens [Relay](https://relay.link) with your address filled in. [Across](https://across.to/?to=robinhood) works too. |
| **Buy with card** | **Open MoonPay** to buy USDG. Not available in New York or Canada. |
| **Official bridge from Ethereum** | Under **Advanced**: the [Arbitrum bridge](https://portal.arbitrum.io/bridge?sourceChain=ethereum&destinationChain=robinhood-chain) from Ethereum to Robinhood Chain. Slower. |

Rushr only links to the official URLs of these services. Watch out for look-alike "Robinhood bridge" sites.
